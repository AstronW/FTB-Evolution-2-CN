# Bee Genes

## Productivity

Productive Bees genes mostly matter for bees out in the world. Inside the apiary, the one that counts is **Productivity**.

A higher Productivity gene means a bee makes more each batch, and it adds on top of the [Productivity upgrade](upgrades.md#productivity).

## Checking a Bee

Hover over a bee in the [Bee Box](modules.md#bee-box) to see its genes.

Breed for higher Productivity to get the most out of your apiary.
