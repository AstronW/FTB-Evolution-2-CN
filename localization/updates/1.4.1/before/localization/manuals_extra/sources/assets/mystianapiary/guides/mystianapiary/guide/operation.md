# Apiary Operation

## Adding Bees

Put filled **Productive Bees cages** into the **Bee Box**. Each cage is one bee, and the apiary runs it right there, so no bees ever fly off.

Take a bee back out by clicking its slot with an empty cage.

## Giving Flowers

Most bees need a certain flower or block to work. Put what they want into the **Flower Box**.

If a bee's flower is missing it simply waits, and starts again the moment you add one.

## Producing

Each bee takes a little while to make its product. A <Color color="#55FF55">green bar</Color> on its slot shows how far along it is.

Add [Speed upgrades](upgrades.md#speed) to the Controller to make them work faster.

## Collecting

Everything the bees make drops into the **Storage**. Pull it out by hand, or with hoppers and pipes.

If the Storage fills up, the bees just pause until there is room again - nothing is ever lost.

## Automation

Hoppers, pipes, and similar can feed the **Bee** and **Flower** boxes and empty the **Storage**, so the whole apiary can run on its own.

Want more from each bee? See [Upgrades](upgrades.md).
